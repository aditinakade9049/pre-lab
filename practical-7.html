Ratan Tata

Visionary Business Leader

About

Ratan Tata is one of India's most respected industrialists and philanthropists. He served as chairman of the Tata Group and led the company to global success.

Biography

Born in 1937, Ratan Tata joined the Tata Group in 1962. Under his leadership, Tata became a global brand by acquiring major international companies.

Important Milestones

  • 1962 – Joined Tata Group
  • 1991 – Became Chairman of Tata Sons
  • 2008 – Acquired Jaguar Land Rover
  • 2012 – Retired as Chairman
